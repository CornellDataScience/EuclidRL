================================================================================
ALGORITHM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

For each training batch:
  1. Sample GROUP of 32 candidate proofs per theorem
  2. Verify each proof with Lean 4 (reward: 1 or 0)
  3. Compute group-relative advantages: reward - group_mean
  4. Update policy to favor high-reward proofs
  5. Apply KL penalty to prevent divergence

Key Equation:
  loss = -(advantages * log_probs).mean() + kl_penalty * kl_divergence

================================================================================
HYPERPARAMETERS (from DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5 Paper, Section 2.3)
================================================================================

  learning_rate:         5e-6    (constant)
  batch_size:            512     (training batch)
  group_size:            32      (candidates per theorem)
  kl_penalty:            0.02    (KL coefficient)
  max_response_length:   2048    (max tokens)
